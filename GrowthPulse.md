## 🌟 Why GrowthPulse?

GrowthPulse is a privacy-first, client-side **Google Chrome Extension (Manifest V3)** designed to help creators, agencies, and brands build authentic, engaged audiences on Instagram without violating platform rate limits or using suspicious automation patterns.

Unlike traditional bots that spam actions and risk account standing, GrowthPulse utilizes **humanized Gaussian pacing**, **24-hour daylight scheduling**, and **automatic safety cool-down engines** to emulate natural browsing behavior.

---

## ✨ Key Features

* **⚡ Smart Engagement Autopilot:** Discover and engage active followers of target niche leaders and competitors with organic like distributions.
* **🛡️ Progressive Safety Strike Ladder:** Automatically detects platform rate limits (HTTP 429 / 400) and imposes reassuring 6h, 12h, or 24h **Smart Account Rest** periods to protect account health.
* **⏰ 24-Hour Organic Daylight Scheduler:** Runs natural morning/evening peak speed multipliers (1.2x–1.5x) and automatically pauses during night rest hours (00:00–08:00).
* **📈 Growth Analytics Studio:** Track your daily follower gain and net audience momentum across **1 Month**, **6 Weeks**, and **1 Year** trend curves.
* **🔄 Multi-Account Profile Isolation:** Seamlessly switches quotas and engagement history when switching between multiple Instagram creator profiles in Chrome.
* **🔒 Privacy-First & Zero Password Sharing:** Operates 100% client-side through active browser session cookies (`ds_user_id`). GrowthPulse never asks for or stores your Instagram password.

## 🚀 Getting Started

1. **Install GrowthPulse:** Load the extension into Google Chrome via `chrome://extensions` (Developer Mode ➔ Load Unpacked).
2. **Open Instagram:** Log into your creator account on [instagram.com](https://www.instagram.com).
3. **Configure Targets:** Open the GrowthPulse popup, select **Auto Mode** (engaging your active followings) or enter specific target accounts (e.g. `@competitor1, @niche_leader`).
4. **Switch Autopilot ON:** GrowthPulse begins organic engagement according to the 24-hour natural schedule.
